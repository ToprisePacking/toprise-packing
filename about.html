Our Story

Leading the Future of PP Woven Packaging

Shandong Toprise Packing Materials Co., Ltd. is a fast-growing quality polypropylene packaging products manufacturer with over 10 years of industry experience. Located in Shandong Province, China, our factory covers 15,680 square meters and is equipped with state-of-the-art production machinery.

We specialize in manufacturing FIBC bulk bags, PP woven bags/sacks, PP woven fabric rolls, firewood ventilated bags, and skip waste bags. Our products are widely used in agriculture, fishery, mining, construction, petrochemical, food, and many other industries worldwide.

With 96 skilled employees and a monthly production capacity of 640 tons, we are committed to delivering world-class packaging solutions to clients across 40+ countries. Our mission is to lead the global competitiveness of Chinese packaging manufacturers.
