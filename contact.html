FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our products and services.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)? +

MOQ varies by product. FIBC bulk bags typically start at 200-500 pieces, PP woven bags at 5,000 pieces, and fabric rolls at 1 ton. Custom orders may have different MOQs - please contact us for details.

Can I get samples before placing a bulk order? +

Yes! We provide samples for quality confirmation. Existing samples are free (you pay shipping), and custom samples may incur a small fee that is refunded upon bulk order confirmation.

What shipping terms do you offer? +

We offer FOB, CIF, and DDP terms. Our logistics team handles all export documentation and can arrange shipping to any port worldwide. We work with reliable freight forwarders to ensure timely delivery.

Do you support OEM/ODM customization? +

Absolutely. We support full customization including size, color, lamination, printing (up to 4 colors), UV treatment, food-grade certification, and special designs like baffle bags or ventilated mesh bags.

What certifications do your products have? +

Our products are ISO 9001 certified, SGS food-grade certified, and meet ISO 21898 standards including 5:1 lift testing. We also hold an Industrial Products Production License.

How long is the production lead time? +

Standard products typically take 15-25 days after sample confirmation. Custom orders may take 25-35 days depending on complexity. We provide weekly production progress reports throughout.
